Botanically Yours is a plant propagation and yard gardening club for people in their 20s and early 30s in St. Petersburg. We swap plants, learn how to keep Florida yards alive, and hang out in the shade while we do it.

What we do

Cutting swaps
Bring a cutting, a pup, a seedling, or a pot you have outgrown. Take home something you did not have this morning.
Yard days
We rotate through members' yards, work out what is growing there, learn how to prune it, and take cuttings from whatever can spare them.
ID walks
Easy walks through St. Pete neighborhoods and parks with a plant ID app, a notebook, and a lot of pointing.

No experience needed. If you have killed a pothos, you are in the right place.

Propagation cheat sheet

Easy wins for a Florida yard. Times are rough. Summer heat and humidity speed rooting up, winter slows it down.

PlantMethodRoots in about
Pothos, philodendronStem cutting with at least one node, in water or damp mix2 to 4 weeks
ColeusTip cutting in water, lower leaves stripped1 to 2 weeks
PentasTip cutting in moist potting mix2 to 4 weeks
Spider plantPin a plantlet into soil while it is still attached, or set it in water1 to 3 weeks
Snake plantDivide the rhizomes (instant), or root leaf sections in soil (slow)Division is immediate, leaf sections take months
BromeliadCut off pups once they reach about a third of the parent's sizeSeveral weeks
PlumeriaLet the cut end dry for a few days, then plant in fast-draining mix and go easy on waterSeveral weeks to a few months

Safety note: wear gloves around oleander and sago palm. Oleander sap is toxic, and sago palm seeds and leaves are dangerous to dogs. Both are common in older St. Pete yards.

Not sure what is growing in your yard? Bring a leaf to a meetup.
